# HOLBERY — Opportunity and Discovery Template

Copy for each candidate; use an anonymized ID. Parent HOLBERY; lifecycle IDEA until evidence supports change.

## Opportunity

ID: ___ | category hypothesis: ___ | owner: UNASSIGNED | date/review: ___
Customer segment: ___; specific daily job: ___; trigger/frequency: ___; current workaround: ___; problem cost/time/error baseline: ___; desired measurable outcome: ___; geography/channel: ___; competing alternatives: ___; why HOLBERY fit: ___; rights/private dependency: ___; exclusion risks: ___.

Separate OBSERVED evidence (source/date/sample) from ASSUMPTION. No invented customer quotes or willingness-to-pay. One priority problem; do not solve all business modules at once.

## Discovery script

Ask: walk me through the last time you did this; where did the task fail; how often; what did you lose/time spent; how do you solve it now; who approves/pay; what would change behavior; how would you judge success? Do not lead with features or promise revenue. Record counterevidence and alternatives, including doing nothing.

## Evidence record

Interview count: ___; anonymized source references: ___ (real identity/consent kept privately); repeated workflow findings: ___; baseline observation: ___; disconfirming findings: ___; operator availability: ___; estimated support/delivery cost: ___; next test: ___.

## Decision gate

Go to EXPERIMENT only with specific customer/job, measurable baseline, accountable owner, timebox/budget cap, consent/privacy approach, and feasibility/rights clear enough for a safe test. HOLD if owner/consent/rights unresolved. Retire if frequency/value too low or existing alternative solves adequately. Decision authority/reference/date: ___.

Use registries/opportunities.csv for sanitized hypotheses and status; personal/company confidential details do not go in git. No opportunity becomes VALIDATED merely from interviews being scheduled.
