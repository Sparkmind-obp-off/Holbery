# HOLBERY — Vertical Expansion Framework

2026-10-06 | Framework IMPLEMENTED; no second vertical launched. This requested legacy Phase9 framework maps to active Roadmap10 Portfolio Engine, not replacement of Roadmap9 Commercial Proof.

## Sequence

Barber first; cafe/laundry/salon/local services/UMKM are candidates, not simultaneous commitments. Require demand → problem → repeatability → unit economics → operational fit → product fit before allocating build effort.

## Reusable core

Reuse pure input validation, calculator/rendering pattern, opt-in field-event contract, privacy boundaries, content loop, template review and existing commerce/delivery capabilities. New vertical-specific layers contain job vocabulary, time/cost assumptions, workflow, rights and support context. Do not duplicate payment truth, databases, identities or parent brands.

## Discovery and demand test

Observe actual work/alternatives; identify one question worth answering repeatedly; test search/content intent before software expansion; validate result comprehension and recording burden; obtain feedback and concrete willingness-to-pay only when a deeper need exists. Do not use barber event volume as proof of cafe demand.

## Go/no-go

Use [scorecard](VERTICAL-SCORECARD.md) and [template](VERTICAL-TEMPLATE.md). Unknown dimensions cause HOLD. Rights/privacy issues cause NO-GO until resolved. GO_TO_EXPERIMENT means a bounded test, not validated venture/child brand. Require first-pattern evidence and capacity before a second market. No traffic buying, unsupported health/accounting advice or speculative AI features.

## Current decision

Hold all new vertical builds. Three barber utilities and the existing product are sufficient to establish usage/feedback/transaction/outcome evidence. Weekly review can suggest a discovery candidate but cannot bypass commercial and portfolio maturity gates.
