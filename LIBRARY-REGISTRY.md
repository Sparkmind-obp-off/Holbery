## Rules
One primary category per item. Lifecycle and maturity are separate. Evidence must distinguish engineering, operational, customer, commercial, economic, rights, security, and portfolio evidence.

Documentation alone cannot upgrade an item to VALIDATED, PROVEN, PRODUCT, or VENTURE.

Before creating a new capability, search this registry and the canonical Library. Reuse by reference when purpose and boundaries match. Create a new item only when function, owner, lifecycle, deployment boundary, or economic role materially differs.

Review after material changes and at least monthly during active work.
