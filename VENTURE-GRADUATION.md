# HOLBERY — Venture / Child Graduation

2026-10-06 | Governance framework, no child brand or venture graduated.

## Gate

Require distinct audience/problem/promise; repeated useful outcome; real demand and positive contribution including support; accountable operator/P&L; feasible independent operating cadence; evidence that parent-native naming limits market value; clean rights/IP/domain/contact; support capacity and strategic separation rationale.

IDEA/EXPERIMENT does not become VENTURE because it has a name, website, deployed calculator or event count. PRODUCT can remain parent-native indefinitely. Utility data supports discovery, never substitutes customer evidence or legal ownership. Default: KEEP HOLBERY-NATIVE when separation adds no clear value.

## Decision record

Evidence window/cohort; source/authority; legal entity/operator/IP owner distinction; investment and support needs; unit economics; dependencies; parent endorsement license; data/consent boundaries; domain/account control; reviewer/decision/date; rollback/retirement trigger. Unknown rights or owner causes HOLD. Do not assume GitHub admin is legal IP owner.

## Spin-off / retirement / exit

Transfer agreements, licenses, code/brand/domain ownership, contracts, support/refund obligations and lawful data handling must be explicit. No automatic rename, company creation, licensing grant or sale. Stop new obligations before retirement; communicate support/fulfillment responsibilities; archive decisions; revoke access safely. Destructive/legal actions require explicit owner approval.
