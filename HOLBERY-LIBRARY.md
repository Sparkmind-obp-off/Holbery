# HOLBERY — Master Library

**Status: CANONICAL FOUNDATION**  
**Role: Parent Library / Single Source of Truth**

HOLBERY Library is the canonical knowledge, capability, asset, and portfolio layer of HOLBERY. It is not merely a file archive and it is not a collection of child brands.

## 1. Purpose

The Library exists to:

- preserve reusable knowledge and intellectual patterns;
- register systems, products, ventures, commerce capabilities, and supporting assets;
- prevent duplicated work across projects;
- make proven capabilities reusable;
- provide the evidence trail for promotion from idea to validated offer;
- keep experimental work clearly separated from commercial reality.

The Library is a **parent layer**. Child brands are a downstream governance outcome, not the default storage model.

## 2. Canonical structure

```text
HOLBERY
├── LIBRARY
│   ├── SYSTEMS
│   ├── PRODUCTS
│   ├── VENTURES
│   ├── COMMERCE
│   ├── KNOWLEDGE
│   ├── CAPABILITIES
│   └── ASSETS
│
└── BRAND PORTFOLIO
    └── CHILD BRANDS
```

### SYSTEMS
Reusable workflows, tools, infrastructure, operating systems, automations, and repeatable execution mechanisms.

Examples: voice-to-voice workflows, runner/execution mechanisms, restore/recovery plans, download/delivery mechanisms, business operating systems.

### PRODUCTS
Packaged, repeatable offers that can be delivered to a customer.

Examples: kits, templates, playbooks, calculators, packaged workflows, digital products.

### VENTURES
Businesses that have their own customer, accountable operator, economics, and operating responsibility.

A project does **not** become a venture because it has a website or a name.

### COMMERCE
Distribution, storefront, partner, affiliate, marketplace, fulfillment, and other commercial-channel capabilities.

### KNOWLEDGE
Research, methods, decisions, experiments, lessons, frameworks, and reusable operating knowledge.

### CAPABILITIES
Cross-cutting capabilities that may support multiple systems or products but do not yet justify an independent product or brand.

### ASSETS
Domains, repositories, websites, design assets, documentation, infrastructure, licenses, and other owned/controlled resources.

## 3. Library is not a child-brand factory

Do not create a child brand simply because a capability has a name.

A Library item may remain:

- HOLBERY-native;
- descriptive;
- internal;
- experimental;
- unbranded;
- or productized under HOLBERY.

A child brand is considered only when independent identity creates real strategic value.

## 4. Promotion lifecycle

```text
IDEA
  ↓
RESEARCH
  ↓
EXPERIMENT
  ↓
PROTOTYPE
  ↓
VALIDATED
  ↓
PRODUCTIZED
  ↓
COMMERCIAL
  ↓
SCALE
  ↓
OPTIONAL CHILD BRAND / INDEPENDENT VENTURE
```

Promotion requires evidence appropriate to the stage. A document, prototype, repository, or test result is not customer validation.

## 5. Child-brand promotion gate

A Library item may be proposed for child-brand treatment only when most of the following are true:

1. distinct customer/audience;
2. distinct problem or promise;
3. meaningful market separation;
4. independent economic potential;
5. accountable owner/operator;
6. reusable operating model;
7. clear reason parent branding is insufficient;
8. preliminary name/domain/handle screening;
9. legal/IP rights can be separated cleanly;
10. continued operation would benefit from an independent identity.

If these conditions are not met, keep the item HOLBERY-native.

## 6. Relationship to HOLBERY

HOLBERY remains the parent identity.

The Library does not compete with HOLBERY and does not become a second master brand.

Preferred relationship:

**HOLBERY → Library → Capability / System / Product / Venture / Commerce → optional Child Brand**

Not:

**HOLBERY → dozens of forced child brands**

## 7. Reuse rules

When starting a new project:

1. search the Library first;
2. reuse an existing capability where practical;
3. extend an existing system instead of duplicating it;
4. create a new item only when the function, ownership, or lifecycle is materially distinct;
5. record the relationship between reused components.

## 8. Boundaries

- Private projects remain excluded unless explicitly authorized.
- Bozq One System remains private/internal and is not imported into the public HOLBERY Library.
- Customer data, credentials, secrets, and private evidence do not belong in the public repository.
- Historical material may inform decisions but does not override canonical current documents.
- Legal ownership and trademark registration are never inferred from Library registration.

## 9. Upgrade policy

The Library is designed to evolve.

Upgrade when there is a real need:

- a new reusable capability appears;
- an existing capability changes materially;
- a project graduates lifecycle stage;
- a child-brand decision becomes justified;
- governance becomes insufficient;
- new commercial evidence changes the architecture.

Do not upgrade merely for cosmetic completeness.

## 10. Definition of a healthy HOLBERY Library

A healthy Library is:

- searchable;
- canonical;
- deduplicated;
- lifecycle-aware;
- evidence-aware;
- rights-aware;
- reusable;
- connected to operating work;
- honest about what is experimental versus proven.

**Principle:** build the Library once, reuse it many times, and let proven value—not naming enthusiasm—determine what becomes a brand.
