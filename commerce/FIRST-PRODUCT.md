## Decision

The first commercial product is a digital, immediately deliverable barber business operating kit, not a generic barber SaaS.

Working product name: **Barber Revenue Starter System**

Promise: help a small barbershop organize pricing, services, repeat customers, daily operations, and follow-up without buying monthly software.

Current Indonesian competitors already cover booking, POS, WhatsApp reminders, commissions, inventory and reporting, with entry pricing around Rp79K–Rp100K/month. Digital SOP/template products are also being sold around Rp99K–Rp120K. citeturn0search0turn0search3turn0search9turn0search12

Therefore the first HOLBERY wedge should not clone POS/booking SaaS. It should be a practical implementation kit that produces an operational result immediately.

## V1 offer

### Core package

1. Barber Numbers Sheet — service price calculator, daily revenue target, cuts/day target, break-even helper, weekly revenue tracker.
2. Service & Pricing System — menu structure, customer segmentation, add-ons, bundles, price review worksheet.
3. Daily Operations System — opening/closing checklist, cash reconciliation, stock/use log, shift handover, complaint log.
4. Customer Repeat System — customer capture, last-visit tracker, repeat segmentation, WhatsApp reminder/win-back templates, simple loyalty mechanism.
5. Owner Dashboard — daily sales, cuts, average ticket, repeat customers, add-on sales, weekly target vs actual.
6. 30-Day Implementation Plan — setup, baseline, retention, upsell/package, review.
7. Quick-start guide — 30–45 minute setup path and weekly owner review.

## Delivery

V1 is digital and low friction:

- Google Sheets / Excel-compatible workbook
- PDF quick-start manual
- editable SOP/checklist documents
- WhatsApp message templates
- one-page owner scorecard

No installation or subscription is required to receive value.

## Pricing hypothesis

- Starter: Rp49.000
- Complete: Rp99.000 — primary first-sale candidate
- Implementation: Rp249.000 — Complete plus guided setup/customization

This is a launch hypothesis, not validated demand.

## First customer

Target: 1 outlet, 1–5 barbers, owner involved in daily operations, walk-in heavy, WhatsApp-based, and willing to improve repeat visits and operational control.

Avoid large chains and complex enterprise requirements initially.

## Sales promise

Do not promise guaranteed revenue growth.

Use:

> In one session, turn a messy barber operation into a simple weekly operating system you can actually run.

## Evolution

V1 Kit → guided implementation → recurring operating system → lightweight barber software → reusable vertical system for other local operators.

The kit is the validation wedge, not a permanent barber-only commitment.
