# HOLBERY Commerce Decisions

1. HOLBERY is a commerce platform, not a single store.
2. Direct commerce comes before marketplace complexity.
3. Portfolio marketplaces are first-class surfaces.
4. Multiple portfolios may exist simultaneously.
5. Children share the commerce core but retain strict data ownership boundaries.
6. Canonical product identity lives in the commerce core.
7. Distribution channels never become the source of truth.
8. Duitku is an adapter, not the business/order model.
9. Payment status is server-authoritative.
10. True multi-seller settlement is later, not a C0 dependency.
11. A child may graduate to its own runtime without breaking portfolio/distribution contracts.
12. Physical, digital and service offers use one commerce model.
13. Public HOLBERY website and commerce backend remain separable.
14. Real transaction evidence outranks feature count.
