# 7. MARKETPLACE POLICY GATE

Before publishing on any marketplace:

1. Verify current marketplace seller rules.
2. Verify whether digital products are allowed.
3. Verify whether downloadable files are allowed.
4. Verify prohibited/off-platform payment rules.
5. Verify external-link restrictions.
6. Verify refund/cancellation rules.
7. Verify tax/invoice requirements.
8. Verify seller identity requirements.
9. Verify customer-data restrictions.
10. Record the result in the repository.

**Never design a marketplace workflow from assumption.**

If a channel does not support the intended digital fulfillment model, do not bypass its rules.

Instead:
- use a compliant product format, or
- use another channel.

# 13. TAX & COMPLIANCE WORKSTREAM

Tax/compliance is a scheduled workstream, not an afterthought.

Operator identity currently documented:

**PT Waskita Cakrawarti Digital — Perseroan Perorangan**

Do not publish sensitive registration numbers unless intentionally authorized.

### Tax/compliance tasks

Schedule and complete:

1. Confirm applicable business tax regime.
2. Confirm whether VAT/PPN obligations apply to the relevant transaction model.
3. Confirm treatment of digital products/services.
4. Confirm marketplace withholding/reporting treatment.
5. Confirm marketplace fee treatment.
6. Confirm payment-provider fee treatment.
7. Confirm invoice/receipt requirements.
8. Confirm accounting treatment of gross sales vs net marketplace payout.
9. Confirm refund/chargeback accounting treatment.
10. Confirm cross-border treatment for international channels such as Etsy.
11. Confirm required bookkeeping evidence.
12. Establish monthly reconciliation.
13. Establish tax-document archive.
14. Review compliance whenever a new channel or business model is activated.

Use qualified Indonesian tax/legal advice for final tax conclusions.

### Monthly reconciliation

At minimum:

```
Marketplace gross sales
- marketplace fees
- payment fees
- refunds
- other documented deductions
= net settlement
```

Then reconcile against:

- HOLBERY order records
- payment records
- marketplace settlement reports
- bank/payment settlement
- accounting/tax records

**Never calculate tax from an unverified dashboard screenshot alone.**
