# HOLBERY — Barber Real Proof Program

**Lifecycle:** EXPERIMENT  
**Current maturity:** L2 BUILT / L3 NOT YET OPERATED

## 1. Proof question

Can a small barber/operator use a simple daily-close workflow repeatedly, with low support burden, to improve reconciliation clarity enough to justify continued use and a legitimate paid offer?

This is narrower than asking whether the whole barber system works.

## 2. Proof stages

### Stage A — Discovery
Target 5 independent owner/operators. Capture current close method, frequency, time, discrepancies, alternatives, and decision consequences.

Gate: repeated problem pattern with a clear job worth testing.

### Stage B — Pilot
Target 2 eligible operators, one location each, 14 open-day window. Requirements: consent, private support channel, baseline, versioned toolkit, onboarding, actual use.

### Stage C — Operational proof
Minimum: at least 90% required-close completeness on scheduled open days; two consecutive usable/reconciled close records per operator; independent operator completes without builder intervention; support burden recorded; no unresolved privacy/rights incident.

This is operational evidence, not PMF.

### Stage D — Commercial proof
After operational gate: present the same defined offer to at least 3 eligible operators; record offers, objections, decisions; seek at least 1 genuine paid commitment; collect only through approved channel/terms; measure delivery time, support, fees, and contribution.

### Stage E — Repeatability
Before calling the offer repeatable: at least 2 legitimate delivery cycles; stable scope/onboarding; support within guardrail; customer outcome recorded; contribution acceptable under real delivery cost; no material rights/privacy issue; documented delivery playbook works.

## 3. Decisions

GO: operational gate met and commercial test is safe.

HOLD: useful signal but sample, support, rights, or definitions incomplete.

ITERATE: outcome useful but workflow friction/cost requires controlled change.

NO-GO: no meaningful outcome, alternative is clearly superior, burden exceeds benefit, or safety/rights cannot be resolved.

## 4. Evidence boundary

Engineering tests are ENGINEERING evidence. Operator observations are CUSTOMER/OPERATIONAL evidence. Paid commitments are COMMERCIAL evidence. Do not combine them into one undifferentiated score.

Public repo stores only anonymized aggregates, evidence references, version, and limitations. Private business records remain private.

## 5. Required artifacts

Discovery log; private consent/reference record; baseline; pilot roster with private IDs; onboarding checklist; version/change log; support log; outcome metrics; commercial offer record; delivery-cost record; decision record; learning/postmortem record.

## 7. Anti-bias rule

Do not recruit only friendly operators, discard negative feedback, change thresholds after results, or count the builder as a customer.

**Success is an independent operator receiving useful value under realistic conditions.**
