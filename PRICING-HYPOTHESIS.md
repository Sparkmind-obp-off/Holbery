# HOLBERY — Barber Pricing Hypothesis

Phase 7 | Hypotheses only; no sales/paid commitments or active checkout.

## First test

Free: daily-close calculator + blank CSV + onboarding instructions. Guided 14-day pilot hypothesis: **Rp149,000 one-time**, one location, one closing workflow, <=90min operator support total. The price is a test anchor, not verified willingness-to-pay or a public payment request. Commercial terms/owner/tax treatment/payment/refund/private contact require approval first.

## Cost model

Hypothesis support time90min × Rp50,000/hour = Rp75,000; estimated payment fee3% = Rp4,470; fulfillment allocationRp5,000 → contributionRp64,530 before acquisition, taxes, fixed overhead and refunds (about43.3%). These are illustrative assumptions, NOT actual costs or profit. If support reaches180min, the offer is likely contribution-negative at this anchor; reduce support burden/scope or revise price, not hide labor cost.

## Validation design

After problem/use test, offer same scope to at least three eligible operators through approved private channel; record offer made, price, objections, acceptance/refusal, deposit/paid commitment only when legal/payment readiness exists. “Sounds good” is not payment evidence. No fabricated conversion. Test delivery/support cost before recurring pricing.

## Pricing guardrails

No automatic subscription. No lifetime unlimited support. No custom software per customer in starter pilot. One included workflow; scope changes are separate offer. No discount ladder that erases labor economics. Clearly disclose experimental nature, exclusions, support cap, refund/service terms and current availability.

## Decision

Go to productization validation when an operator understands outcome/delivery and at least one real paid commitment can be accepted safely. Adjust on actual cost/WTP evidence; do not treat the free calculator as validated paid demand. Recurring workflow support may be explored only if operator use persists and ongoing value justifies recurring cost.
