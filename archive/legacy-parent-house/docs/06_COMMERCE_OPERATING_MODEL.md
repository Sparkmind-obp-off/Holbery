# 06 — COMMERCE OPERATING MODEL

## 1. Commerce principle
HOLBERY should maintain one operational truth while distributing through many channels.

```
Canonical Product Source
        ↓
Commerce Engine
        ↓
Orders / Payments / Customers / Inventory / Fulfillment
        ↓
Channels
├── Own Store
├── Shopee
├── TikTok Shop
├── Tokopedia
├── Social Commerce
├── Affiliate
├── Reseller
└── B2B
```

## 2. Canonical entities
The commerce engine should normalize:
- Product
- SKU
- Variant
- Price
- Inventory
- Customer
- Order
- Payment
- Fulfillment
- Channel
- Campaign
- Partner / reseller

## 3. Parent vs child ownership
HOLBERY owns shared standards and infrastructure.

Child brands own:
- customer proposition
- offer design
- product assortment
- campaign decisions
- category content

## 4. Distribution principle
One product definition, many distribution endpoints.

Channel-specific copy, pricing, and campaign rules may vary, but operational truth should remain centralized.

## 5. Commerce phases

### Phase 1
Own-store and manual operations.

### Phase 2
Marketplace synchronization and better order normalization.

### Phase 3
Affiliate, reseller, and B2B tooling.

### Phase 4
Cross-brand portfolio analytics and shared customer intelligence.

## 6. Revenue logic
Track gross sales, net sales, contribution margin, CAC, repeat rate, channel mix, returns, and fulfillment cost by brand and channel.

## 7. Data rule
Customer and transaction data must be modeled once, with clear ownership and consent boundaries.
