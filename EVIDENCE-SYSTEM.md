# HOLBERY — Evidence System

**Status: CANONICAL EVIDENCE GOVERNANCE**

## Evidence hierarchy
REAL TRANSACTION / PAID COMMITMENT → REPEAT USAGE → OBSERVED USER OUTCOME → CONTROLLED PILOT → DIRECT INTERVIEW → EXPERIMENTAL RESPONSE → INTERNAL TEST → SYNTHETIC TEST → HYPOTHESIS

Engineering claims use engineering evidence; legal claims use rights evidence. Do not substitute one class for another.

## Evidence record
Each material claim traces to claim, source, date/window, sample, method, version, observed result, limitations, privacy boundary, and decision influenced.

## Evidence classes
ENGINEERING, OPERATIONAL, CUSTOMER, COMMERCIAL, ECONOMIC, LEGAL/RIGHTS, SECURITY, PORTFOLIO.

## Claim states
HYPOTHESIS → TESTED → OBSERVED → REPLICATED → PROVEN_FOR_SCOPE → INVALIDATED / SUPERSEDED

Proven is always scoped to context and sample.

## Rules
No fabricated rows. Synthetic data stays synthetic. Customer-identifiable evidence remains private. Negative evidence is preserved. Sample and limitations are visible. Evidence expires when assumptions materially change.

## Decision thresholds
Define thresholds before observation. If not met, choose HOLD, ITERATE, or NO-GO rather than moving the goalpost.

## Graduation package
Problem evidence; user/usage evidence; outcome evidence; commercial evidence when relevant; delivery/support evidence; economic evidence; rights/data evidence; limitations; authority and date.

**Principle:** evidence is the bridge between what HOLBERY says and what HOLBERY knows.
