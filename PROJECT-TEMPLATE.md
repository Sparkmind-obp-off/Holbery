## Planning

Milestone → responsible role → timebox → artifact → test/evidence → next decision. Prioritize customer outcome before automation. Do not introduce SaaS accounts or database unless real delivery needs them. If owner or safe operation unavailable, status is BLOCKED/REQUIRES USER ACTION, not silently LIVE.
