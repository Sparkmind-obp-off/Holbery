# 13 — MASTER PARENT CONTROL PLANE

## Purpose
This defines what makes HOLBERY a real master parent rather than simply another brand name.

## 1. The four parent powers

### A. Ownership
HOLBERY is the strategic owner/controller of the portfolio structure.

Parent-level ownership should cover, as applicable:
- trademarks and brand assets
- domains and parent identity
- shared software/platform IP
- portfolio records
- group-level contracts
- shared operational standards

Legal ownership must be implemented through the appropriate legal entity and professional advice.

### B. Governance
HOLBERY decides:
- which child brands enter the portfolio
- what each child is allowed to own
- naming standards
- parent endorsement rules
- shared-system requirements
- portfolio review cadence
- when a brand should be paused, merged, sold, or retired

### C. Shared capabilities
HOLBERY provides reusable capabilities where economically justified:
- commerce
- finance / reporting
- data
- customer infrastructure
- technology
- distribution
- procurement
- operations
- brand governance

A child brand should consume shared capability rather than rebuild the same infrastructure unnecessarily.

### D. Portfolio capital / resources
Over time, HOLBERY becomes the allocator of:
- capital
- engineering effort
- distribution capacity
- marketing resources
- inventory capacity
- strategic partnerships

Resource allocation follows portfolio performance and strategic value.

## 2. The parent should NOT own everything customer-facing
A master parent does not need to appear on every product page.

Use HOLBERY prominently for:
- corporate site
- group communications
- partnerships
- legal/corporate materials
- careers
- portfolio reporting
- parent-level trust

Use child brands prominently for:
- customer proposition
- category marketing
- product pages
- campaign creative
- category-specific community

## 3. Parent operating model

```
HOLBERY
│
├── Portfolio Governance
│
├── Shared Platform
│   ├── Commerce
│   ├── Data
│   ├── Technology
│   ├── Operations
│   └── Reporting
│
├── Child Brands
│   ├── Brand A
│   ├── Brand B
│   └── Brand C
│
└── Group Development
    ├── New Ventures
    ├── Partnerships
    └── Capital Allocation
```

## 4. Master-parent test
HOLBERY is functioning as a master parent when:
1. multiple child brands can operate without becoming visually identical;
2. HOLBERY can own the shared infrastructure;
3. new brands enter through a common gate;
4. portfolio decisions happen at parent level;
5. the parent can remain credible even when a child brand becomes much larger in its category;
6. the business can sell or retire a child without destabilizing the parent identity.

## 5. North-star definition
**HOLBERY = the house that owns, governs, enables, and grows the portfolio.**
