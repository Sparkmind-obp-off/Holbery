# 07 — NAMING AND CLEARANCE GOVERNANCE

## 1. HOLBERY status
**Strategic status:** MASTER PARENT BRAND LOCK  
**Legal status:** NOT LEGALLY CLEARED

The strategic lock means the portfolio is designed around HOLBERY unless a material clearance failure occurs.

## 2. Naming process
For every future brand:

`Generate → Pre-screen → Deep-screen → Finalist → Clearance → Launch`

## 3. Minimum screening
Check:
- exact spelling
- phonetic similarity
- visual similarity
- active companies
- active products / brands
- trademark databases
- domain availability
- social handles
- linguistic meaning
- adversarial risks

## 4. Priority trademark territories
At minimum review:
- Indonesia / DJKI
- WIPO Global Brand Database
- USPTO
- EUIPO
- UKIPO

Typical high-relevance classes for software/commerce parent work include 9, 35, 36, and 42, plus product-specific classes as needed.

## 5. Status language
Use:
- `PROVISIONALLY CLEAR` — no material issue found in screening, but not legal advice.
- `HOLD` — plausible but unresolved risk remains.
- `DROP` — material collision or strategic weakness.
- `LOCK CANDIDATE` — only after deep screening and business approval.
- `LOCKED` — strategic decision; still subject to legal registration reality.

Never say `legally clear` from web search alone.

## 6. Parent brand change rule
Changing HOLBERY should require a documented reason such as:
- material trademark conflict
- irreparable domain/identity problem
- strategic portfolio mismatch
- severe linguistic issue

Aesthetic boredom is not sufficient reason to restart naming.
