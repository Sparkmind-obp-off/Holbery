# HOLBERY — Barber Customer Problem

Phase 7 | Hypothesis brief. HOLBERY is the parent; barber is first test, not master positioning.

## Problem hypothesis

A small owner-operated barber may close using cash counting, payment app totals, and informal notes. Without a consistent close record, differences can remain unexplained and weekly decisions become guesswork. No actual operator interview has yet confirmed this for the target segment.

## User / buyer / trigger

User: designated closer. Buyer: owner who values reconciliation and operating clarity. Trigger: end of open day; weekly owner review. Alternatives: notebook, spreadsheet, existing POS/merchant dashboards, accountant, or doing nothing. If existing tools solve sufficiently, do not sell duplicate infrastructure.

## Problems to investigate, not assert

Missing service counts, mixed payment modes, cash expenses not recorded, uncertain opening float, reconciliation time, lack of consistent review. Retention, staffing and pricing may matter but are deliberately outside first MVP. Ask for last real close, exact workaround, frequency, cost/time, and next action from the data.

## Discovery questions

Walk through yesterday’s close. Who enters counts and cash? Where is the receipt total obtained? What happened on the last discrepancy? How long until explained? Which information changes a decision? What would make a second system not worth using? What amount/time can be justified for a simpler close? Who approves budget and private records?

## Evidence record / safeguards

Capture actual observation/date/anonymized ID, baseline time, incomplete records, discrepancy context and alternative comparison. Consent/reference and actual private business figures stay in owner-approved private system, not this public repository. Do not copy Bozq data or treat its private history as demand evidence for a new offer.

## Go / no-go

Proceed if multiple independent operators report a repeated valuable job and can safely test it. Hold if bookkeeping definitions differ or ownership/contact/consent unresolved. No-go if existing POS suffices, pain infrequent, or logging burden exceeds benefit. Primary outcome is usability/reconciliation, not revenue increase.
