# FIRST-SALE SPRINT — Barber Revenue Starter System
Status: EXECUTION PLAN / NOT SALES EVIDENCE
Owner constraint: zero spend for testing; never fabricate buyers, payments, or conversion evidence.
Source of truth: existing HOLBERY repo and production storefront. Do not create a new brand, store, database, or product before validation.

## Objective
Earn the first genuine customer payment for the existing Barber Revenue Starter System (BRS-2026-V1), or gather enough evidence to revise/stop the offer. Target window: 14 days; first 72 hours are validation, not a guaranteed sale.

## Hard guardrails
1. Do not import Bosku/Bozq private assets, customer information, operating data, or intellectual property.
2. Do not pay for ads, new domains, APIs, subscriptions, or marketplace listings during this experiment.
3. Do not claim sales, customer outcomes, verified payment, or testimonials until independently evidenced.
4. Do not change production price, catalog, payment configuration, secrets, or checkout code during validation without a documented reason and owner confirmation.
5. Never collect sensitive customer or shop financial data for research. Ask about workflow and pain, not exact private revenue.
6. If checkout fails or payment cannot be verified, record the blocker honestly; do not treat a screenshot, redirect, mock, or readiness response as payment.

## Target buyer and promise
Buyer hypothesis: owner/operator of a 1–5 chair barbershop who struggles to track daily cash, service mix, barber performance, repeat customers, or weekly targets.
Offer hypothesis: a ready-to-use Indonesian-language operating toolkit that helps the owner close the day, understand revenue targets and break-even, and standardize simple operating routines without installing a complex app.
Do not promise a revenue increase. Promise tools, visibility, and repeatable routines.

## First 72 hours
### Day 1 — Verify the buying path (60–90 minutes)
- Open the live storefront in a private browser and inspect the product, price, terms, refund/delivery/support pages, and mobile usability.
- Run only non-purchase browsing checks. Do not create an owner-funded test order.
- Record whether the product is actually listed, whether the price and package contents are accurate, and the exact blocker if checkout readiness or payment initiation fails.
- Prepare one screenshot or short screen recording of a free calculator and one sample/preview page from the paid package that does not expose the full paid asset.

### Day 1 — Publish a problem-first demonstration (90 minutes)
Use the existing HOLBERY identity and existing free tools. Publish one helpful post to a relevant community where promotion is allowed; no mass posting and no unsolicited bulk DMs.

Suggested post:
"Pemilik barbershop: kalau tutup kas harian, cara kalian mencocokkan jumlah layanan, uang masuk, dan pengeluaran bagaimana? Aku sedang menguji alat pencatatan sederhana berbahasa Indonesia untuk daily close, target omzet, dan break-even. Aku ingin memahami alur yang benar-benar dipakai sebelum mengembangkan lebih jauh. Kalau bersedia, ceritakan bagian yang paling merepotkan—nggak perlu membagikan angka keuangan pribadi."

Include a free calculator link only where community rules allow. Do not call this a proven solution.

### Day 2 — Interview 5 relevant operators
Ask 5 owners/operators through existing relationships or opt-in community replies:
1. How do you currently close and check the day's cash?
2. What part is most error-prone or time-consuming?
3. When did this last cause a real problem?
4. What have you tried so far?
5. Would a blank template, guided toolkit, or simple app fit your current workflow best?
6. May I show you a small prototype and ask for honest feedback?
Record anonymized notes; do not invent responses. Avoid pitching before understanding the workflow.

### Day 3 — Make a clear offer to qualified interested buyers
Only after showing the actual contents and limitations:
- Product: Barber Revenue Starter System (BRS-2026-V1).
- Current price: verify live catalog before quoting; the documented hypothesis is Rp99.000.
- Explain exactly what files are included, who it is for, what it does not do, and how delivery/support works.
- Ask a clear, low-pressure question: "Apakah paket ini cukup berguna untuk dibeli pada harga yang tertera, atau apa yang masih membuatnya belum layak?"
- If a buyer is ready, guide them through the live checkout. Count revenue only after the provider/payment state is server-verified and delivery succeeds.

## Days 4–7 — Learn, don't add features
- Seek 10 total relevant conversations and 3 explicit offer reactions.
- Track funnel counts: people reached in relevant contexts, replies, qualified conversations, demos, checkout attempts, verified payments, refunds/support requests.
- Use only real observations; zero is a valid result.
- Fix copy or product clarity first. Build a feature only when at least two independent buyers identify the same important gap.
- If the checkout is blocked, prioritize the smallest safe fix to the existing commerce path rather than creating another storefront.

## Days 8–14 — Decision gate
Continue if there is at least one verified paid order, or strong evidence such as multiple qualified buyers asking to purchase at the displayed price and a specific fixable blocker.
Revise if people recognize the problem but do not understand the package or price.
Stop/pivot if 10 relevant conversations produce no repeated pain and no serious purchase interest.
A sale is not guaranteed by the deadline.

## Proof standard
- Interest: person explicitly asks for more information.
- Qualified demand: target buyer explains a current problem and requests a solution.
- Purchase intent: buyer confirms the specific product and displayed price are acceptable.
- Sale: provider-confirmed successful payment plus a corresponding order record.
- Outcome: customer independently reports actual use/result; keep separate from sale evidence.
