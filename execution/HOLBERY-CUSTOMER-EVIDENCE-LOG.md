**Status:** ACTIVE TEMPLATE

This document is a structured log for customer discovery, product use, transactions, feedback and outcomes.

It exists to prevent memory-based storytelling and unsupported claims.

## Rules

- Record facts, not hopes.
- Do not fabricate a prospect or customer.
- Do not record unnecessary personal data.
- Do not commit private contact details, payment credentials, or sensitive order data.
- A compliment is not a sale.
- A sale is not automatically an outcome.
- An outcome is not automatically repeatability.
- Update the evidence ledger after meaningful evidence is obtained.

## Decision Gate

After meaningful evidence is collected, answer:

1. Is the problem real?
2. Is the problem painful enough to pay to solve?
3. Does the current product solve enough of it?
4. Is Rp99.000 acceptable relative to perceived value?
5. Is delivery friction low enough?
6. What should change in the next version?
7. Should HOLBERY continue, reposition, or stop this product?

## Privacy

This repository is not a customer CRM.

Keep raw PII, private conversations, payment information, and private support records outside GitHub.

This log stores only the minimum evidence necessary for product and strategic decisions.

## Evidence Integrity

Never upgrade evidence merely because time passed.

The state changes only when a real event occurs.

```
INTENTION
→ ARTIFACT
→ VERIFICATION
→ EXTERNAL USE
→ TRANSACTION
→ OUTCOME
→ REPEATABILITY
→ DURABLE ASSET
```
