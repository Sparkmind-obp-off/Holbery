# HOLBERY — Commercial Manual

**Status: CANONICAL COMMERCIAL PLAYBOOK**

## Commercial loop
PROBLEM → CUSTOMER → OUTCOME → OFFER → PRICE → ACQUISITION → SALE → DELIVERY → SUPPORT → MEASURE → RETAIN / REFER / IMPROVE

## Customer
Define user, buyer, decision maker, trigger, eligibility, and disqualifiers. Segment claims require external evidence.

## Problem
Confirm the job through real conversation or observation. Capture workaround, frequency, cost/time, and consequence.

## Outcome
Define a concrete customer-visible outcome. Avoid unsupported guarantees.

## Offer
Specify scope, exclusions, format, delivery time, support, terms, and success metric. Prefer a small repeatable offer before custom implementation.

## Pricing
Price is a hypothesis until real market behavior confirms it. Include labor, support, fees, acquisition, refunds, and capacity.

## Acquisition
Track source → qualified conversation → offer → commitment → delivery. Traffic alone is not demand proof.

## Sales
Interest is not revenue. A paid commitment must be attributable to a real customer and an approved offer.

## Delivery and support
Track delivery time, support minutes, defects, scope changes, and customer outcome. Excessive custom work means the offer needs redesign before scaling.

## Economics
Measure collected revenue, variable delivery/support cost, fees, refunds, acquisition cost where paid, contribution, and capacity/time.

## Commercial gates
Offer-ready → market-tested → paid-proof → repeatable → scalable.

## Guardrails
No fabricated customers, testimonials, revenue, conversion, scarcity, ownership, or legal status. Stop if terms, payment rights, or legal authority are unclear.

**Principle:** money is evidence, not the only evidence; repeated customer value and sustainable delivery matter.
