## Update protocol

Record date, exact scope, source and limitations. Technical control, beneficial/legal owner, operator and custodian are separate fields. Do not publish account identifiers, registrant PII, recovery keys or unrelated project inventories. Evidence cloudflare-discovery stores counts and relevant exact-domain result only.

Review assets monthly and before launch, transfer, new license, collaborator access, or exit. Verify 2FA, billing contact, renewal, recovery, and account access with owner; never pretend these were checked from deployment token alone. Check licenses at release; keep attribution with packaged distributions.

## Required owner actions

Confirm legal name/entity and asset owner; register/control holberry.biz; supply verified business contact; complete trademark review; approve product-delivery terms; assign operating/product owner; recruit real operator pilot. Each action is separable: lack of a domain does not invalidate code or templates, but does prevent claiming the strategic domain is live.
