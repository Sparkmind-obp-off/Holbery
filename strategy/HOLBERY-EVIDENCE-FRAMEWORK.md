# HOLBERY EVIDENCE FRAMEWORK

**Status:** PRIMARY EVIDENCE STANDARD  
**Version:** V1.0  
**Date:** 2026-10-06

## 1. Why Evidence Matters

HOLBERY must distinguish between what we built, what we believe, and what the market has actually demonstrated.

Evidence is the bridge.

## 2. Evidence Hierarchy

### E0 — Intention
"We plan to..."

Not proof.

### E1 — Artifact
A product, page, system, workflow, or prototype exists.

Proof of execution only.

### E2 — Verification
Tests, security checks, calculations, delivery checks, or operational verification pass.

Proof that the system behaves as tested.

### E3 — External Use
A real person uses the product or system.

Proof of use, not necessarily value.

### E4 — Transaction
A real customer pays.

Proof of willingness to exchange money.

### E5 — Outcome
The customer reports or demonstrates a useful result.

Proof of value is stronger.

### E6 — Repeatability
Multiple independent customers, repeat purchases, referrals, retention, or repeated outcomes appear.

Proof that the thesis may be repeatable.

### E7 — Durable Asset
The repeated value becomes a recognizable product, system, venture, or brand capability.

This is the long-term objective.

## 3. Evidence Rules

1. Never call engineering tests "customer validation."
2. Never call readiness "revenue."
3. Never call an invoice "a paid sale" until payment is actually verified.
4. Never call traffic "demand" without behavioral evidence.
5. Never manufacture testimonials.
6. Never manufacture customer records.
7. Separate internal evidence from external evidence.
8. Record negative evidence, not only positive evidence.

## 4. Evidence Ledger

Every major product should maintain:

- claim;
- evidence level;
- source;
- date;
- scope;
- limitations;
- next evidence required.

Example:

| Claim | Level | Evidence | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Checkout works | E2 | production integration tests | not customer demand |
| Product can be delivered | E2 | fulfillment verification | no customer yet |
| Customer wants it | E4+ | real payment | requires actual customer |
| Product creates outcome | E5 | customer outcome | needs independent confirmation |

## 5. Minimum Product Proof

Before a product is treated as strategically proven:

- real problem documented;
- product delivered;
- real user interaction;
- customer feedback;
- transaction if commercial;
- outcome evidence where applicable;
- lessons documented.

## 6. Brand Evidence

HOLBERY brand claims must also have evidence.

Examples:

"Reliable" → demonstrate reliable delivery.

"Useful" → demonstrate actual user value.

"Commerce infrastructure" → demonstrate working commerce infrastructure.

"Parent brand" → demonstrate a coherent portfolio, not merely a logo.

## 7. Evidence Gate

A project may advance only when the evidence required for its current stage exists.

This prevents roadmap theater.
