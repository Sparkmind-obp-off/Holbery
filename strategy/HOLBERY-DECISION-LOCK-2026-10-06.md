# HOLBERY — Strategic Decision Lock

**Date:** 2026-10-06  
**Status:** LOCKED

## Decision

HOLBERY is officially **BRAND-FIRST, PROOF-DRIVEN, COMMERCE-BACKED**.

## Meaning

The long-term asset is HOLBERY.

Products, systems, commerce, distribution, and ventures are mechanisms for creating and compounding value under the parent.

## Priority

1. Direction
2. Value
3. Evidence
4. Trust
5. Transaction
6. Economics
7. Scale

Financial survival remains mandatory. This ordering does not authorize unsustainable spending or ignoring cashflow.

## Rejected primary strategy

**Marketplace-first / transaction-first growth** is not the canonical HOLBERY strategy.

Marketplace and distribution channels remain available as secondary acquisition capabilities.

## First execution objective

Prove one complete value loop:

**Problem → Product → Real User → Transaction → Delivery → Feedback → Evidence → Iteration**

The initial proof vehicle is BRS-2026-V1.

## Architectural consequence

Technical work must support:
- product truth;
- transaction truth;
- delivery truth;
- evidence truth;
- customer learning;
- portfolio governance.

Technical complexity without strategic value is rejected.

## Reopening conditions

This decision may be revisited only if:
- market evidence materially contradicts the strategy;
- legal/IP constraints materially change it;
- financial survival requires a temporary tactical adaptation;
- a new venture/product thesis creates a materially stronger parent strategy.

A temporary tactical change must not silently become a new master strategy.
