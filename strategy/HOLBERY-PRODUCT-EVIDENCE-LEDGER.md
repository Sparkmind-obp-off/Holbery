## Definition of Honest Progress

Progress can be real even when revenue is zero.

Examples:
- clearer problem;
- better product;
- stronger evidence;
- better fulfillment;
- better positioning;
- customer learning.

However, the business must eventually convert value into sustainable economics.

**Brand-first is not revenue-free forever. It is value-first with commercial discipline.**
