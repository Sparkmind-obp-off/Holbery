2026-10-06 | Framework IMPLEMENTED, demand/outcome proof PENDING. Extends existing approved BRS product; does not create a new paid product or override commerce/payment truth.

## Ladder

FREE utility → useful repeated work → clearer problem → relevant existing product → verified transaction/delivery → outcome → only then broader operating system. Free means discovery and standalone utility; paid means continuous operational value, implementation or packaged work. All three calculators stay usable without signup, data capture or purchase.

## Current offer

The existing Barber Revenue Starter System COMPLETE BRS-2026-V1 is a private licensed package at Rp99.000 in canonical D1 catalog, with protected R2 fulfillment. Do not copy its workbook/PDFs into public templates or infer another approval requirement already satisfied by the owner. Public CSVs are newly authored blank utility resources, not the whole paid operating system.

## Product gate

Wait for real traffic, consented use, repeated short-session use, specific requested improvement, product interest and genuine paid commitment. Event counts only locate uncertainty; human feedback confirms a problem, verified provider/server evidence confirms payment, and actual use confirms outcome. Thresholds must be declared per experiment, not retrofitted to favorable results.

## Next-product policy

Pricing/simulator/snapshot remain candidates. No new paid product, marketplace, CRM or giant SaaS now. Compare tool completion/friction and actual feedback weekly. If a utility gets little use, improve discoverability/usability before multiplying tools. Any paid graduation needs clear customer/problem/outcome/format/price/channel/delivery/support/margin, plus rights and repeatable delivery. No monetization deadline after30 days.
